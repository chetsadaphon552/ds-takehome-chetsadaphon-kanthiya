PROBLEM 2.A1 - INDIRECT OWNERSHIP AND THE NEUMANN SERIES

Notation
--------
M_ij is the fraction of company j held directly by company i. The matrix power
M^r records ownership paths of length r, with the usual matrix multiplication
summing the contributions of paths through intermediate companies. Total
ownership excluding the direct identity term is:

    T = M + M^2 + M^3 + ...

Proof of the inverse formula
----------------------------
Define the finite partial sum:

    S_N = I + M + M^2 + ... + M^N

Multiplying by (I - M) gives a telescoping sum:

    S_N (I - M) = I - M^(N+1)

If the matrix geometric series converges, then M^(N+1) tends to the zero
matrix. Taking the limit yields:

    S (I - M) = I
    S = (I - M)^(-1)

Since T excludes the identity term:

    T = S - I = (I - M)^(-1) - I

Convergence condition
---------------------
For a finite square matrix, the Neumann series sum_{r=0}^infinity M^r
converges if and only if the spectral radius satisfies:

    rho(M) = max_i |lambda_i(M)| < 1

Real-world interpretation
-------------------------
An ownership matrix is non-negative. If every company is at most 100% owned by
the set of companies represented in the matrix, its columns have sums at most
one. This makes the matrix column-substochastic and implies rho(M) <= 1.
Strict convergence requires that there is no closed ownership component with
no leakage to shareholders outside the represented network; in particular,
each strongly connected closed component must have some ownership leakage.
Public float, individuals, government, or entities omitted from the registry
often provide such leakage. This is a practical reason convergence is common,
not a guarantee: a fully owned closed cycle can have rho(M) = 1 and must be
handled explicitly.

Assessment matrix check
-----------------------
For the given four-company matrix, the non-zero 3-by-3 ownership block contains
the cycle A -> B -> C -> A with product 0.6 * 0.5 * 0.2 = 0.06. The computed
spectral radius is approximately 0.3915, which is less than one, so the series
converges. The hand calculation and NumPy calculation of T are shown in
src/q2/1_ownership_pca.ipynb. They give A's total ownership of D as
0.22 / 0.94 = 0.2340, or 23.40%.
